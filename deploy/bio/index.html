Overview

Debangsha Sarkar is a computer scientist and machine learning practitioner based in Burnaby, in Metro Vancouver. He holds two master’s degrees from the University of British Columbia: an M.Sc. in Computer Science and a Master of Data Science. His published research concerns representative sampling and active learning. Since April 2024 he has been Chief Technology Officer of EclimAi Ltd in Dublin.
